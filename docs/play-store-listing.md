# Play Store listing (draft)

## App name
Beatpath: Learn music with Lou

## Short description (80 characters max)
Learn notes, chords and beats from zero, then make your own songs. With Lou!

## Full description
Beatpath teaches music from the very first note. Lou, a friendly creature with a speaker for a belly, guides you all the way to writing your own songs. Every lesson is short and made for beginners. If you're 8 or 80, you can start today.

Start from zero
• Find any note on a rainbow keyboard. Every note has its own color.
• Learn octaves, half steps, sharps and flats, and how to work out any note by counting keys.
• Build scales and chords with simple recipes, like "take, skip, take".
• Find out which note to play next, and why some notes feel like home.

Then make real music
• Make beats in a Channel Rack and draw melodies in a Piano roll. They work just like the ones in music software such as FL Studio.
• Write chords, bass lines and melodies in 22 levels: grooves, harmony, song form, and lo-fi, house, trap and pop.
• Chain loops A, B and C into a whole song in the song maker, then export it as MIDI or WAV.

Play with Lou
• Five mini games that train real skills:
  - Sky Hop: hear if a note goes up or down
  - Beat Run: keep the beat
  - Balloon Pop: name the notes
  - Chord Cake: build chords
  - Echo Lou: remember tunes
• Daily quests, streak chests and XP to spend in Lou's Shop: hats, glasses, scarves, colors and mystery boxes.

Made to be safe
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed.
• Everything stays on your phone.
