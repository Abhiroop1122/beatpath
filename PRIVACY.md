# Beatpath privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Beatpath is a music-learning app for Android. This policy explains what it does with information about you. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

## What the app stores

All of this stays on your phone, inside the app's own private storage:

- lessons you've finished
- your XP, level and streak
- quest progress and mini game best scores
- the outfit Lou is wearing
- your settings
- the loops and songs you make

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

## What the app sends

Nothing. Beatpath has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no network permission. It does not collect, sell or share personal information.

## Crash reports

If the app closes by accident, it writes a short technical report to a file on your phone. The next time you open the Me tab, it asks whether you want to share that report or delete it.

Sharing opens Android's normal share sheet. You choose which app sends it and to whom, for example email. Nothing is sent unless you do that. The report contains:

- the app version
- the Android version
- the phone model
- the technical error trace

It contains no lesson data and no music you made.

## Exports

When you export a loop as MIDI or WAV, Android asks where to save it. The file goes only where you choose.

## Children

Beatpath is designed to be usable by children. Because it collects nothing and has no network access, no personal information about a child ever leaves the device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with a new date.
